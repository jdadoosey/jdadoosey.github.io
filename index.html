Jeffrey Daniel-Adoosey

PHOTOGRAPHER | PRODUCT DESIGNER

I am a self-taught photographer and product designer. For ten years I have told people’s stories through images, and for the last four, I have designed software that people use every day, from an enterprise system for a pharmaceutical manufacturer to consumer apps. The harder the problem, the more I enjoy finding the solution.

This portfolio brings both practices together. The photography section covers commercial, portraiture, headshots, and weddings shot across Ghana, the UK, and the US. The product design section presents four projects: an enterprise ERP system, an AI powered live audio platform, a consumer mobile app, and a set of client websites. I chose them because they involved real clients, real constraints, and very different design problems, and because they show how I think from problem to solution, not only what the final screens look like.
